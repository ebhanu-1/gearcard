High-frequency flicker reduction

Fine-step shutter speeds to remove LED banding stripes (modes S and M).

ON YOUR Z8 Photo shooting menu › High-frequency flicker reduction
STAGE DANCE IN LOW LED LIGHT On. Start near your target speed (1/320–1/500 s) and nudge until the bands vanish in magnified view. Recheck at every lighting cue.

WHAT IT DOES

Lets you set shutter speed in tiny steps (1/500 shows as 512.0) so it matches the PWM frequency of LED stage lights or screens, and the stripes disappear. You watch the magnified live view and nudge until they're gone.

OPTIONS

OnSpeed shown with one decimal; fine steps. Use under LED rigs, LED walls and LED-lit halls.
OffStandard 1/3-stop speeds. Everything else.

OTHER SCENARIOS

Kids indoorsOn only if the LEDs band
MacroOff, unless under an LED panel
Birds, landscape, moonriseOff

GOTCHAS

The live view can differ from the file — take test shots. A lighting change between cues can bring banding back. Turning it off rounds the speed to the nearest standard value.

BODY DIFFERENCES

Z8 firmware 3.00 added frequency presets. The Z8's 3.7 ms readout reduces banding but doesn't remove it. Z50 II range is 1/4000–1/30 s (1/2000 with EFCS).

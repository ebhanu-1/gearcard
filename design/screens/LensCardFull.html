AVOID

Past f/11 displayed with the TC on the Z8, unless you need the depth. TC wide open (f/4) when you have light to spare — f/5.6 is clearly crisper. The TC-2.0x on this lens for critical detail.

CHARACTER

CAUnder ~1 px lateral, axial essentially absent — clean moon limbs.
BokehNeutral, smooth transitions; cat's-eye highlights at 200 mm f/2.8.
FlareSome ghosting into the sun, worse at 200 mm. Keep the hood on.
VignettingUp to ~1.6 stops at 200 mm wide open; gone by f/5.6.
FocusNo breathing; not parfocal — refocus after zooming.

FIELD TIPS

1Moonrise at 280 mm: shoot displayed f/5.6–f/8. The moon is about 2.5 mm on the sensor (≈580 px on the Z8), so plan to crop.
2On a tripod, focus manually on the moon's limb at full magnification; focus holds when you stop down.
3For long bursts, VR Sport keeps framing steady; Normal can jump between frames as it recentres.

ON THE Z8

Most lab data is from 45MP bodies, so the verdicts apply directly. Diffraction becomes visible from about f/8 displayed. Synchro VR works with lens firmware 1.20 or later.
